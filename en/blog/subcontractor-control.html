Guide

Subcontractor control without paper chaos

How to control subcontractors on site: digital ID, gate check, and live attendance instead of handwritten lists.

The real pain

Who is on site, for which company, and since when? Lists and phone calls do not scale when trades and subcontractors change daily.

What works better

  • Physical card or app pass
  • Gate with photo and company
  • Live map for site leads
  • Reports for clients and payroll

Practical start

  • One pilot site
  • Cards for people without smartphones
  • Gate process agreed with security/reception
  • 30-minute demo before rollout
