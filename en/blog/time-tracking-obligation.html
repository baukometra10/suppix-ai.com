Whether and how time tracking is required: overview for construction and industry – and why digital systems replace Excel and punch clocks.

Employers need to record working time in a way that can be verified. Court rulings, audits, or payroll: unclear lists cost time and money.

What goes wrong in practice

What a modern system must deliver

FAQ

Is time tracking mandatory in Germany?

The duty to systematically record working time has been strengthened – companies should have reliable processes. Not legal advice: check your case with a tax advisor/lawyer.

Is Excel enough?

For small, stable teams short-term maybe – with sites, subcontractors, and multiple locations usually not.
