QR, NFC and GPS geofence compared: which check-in mode fits construction sites, halls, and field teams.

Three paths, one system

Not every site needs the same check-in. WorkPass combines QR, NFC and GPS – plus cards without a smartphone requirement.

Quick comparison

  • QR: fast at fixed points
  • NFC: card/badge at a reader
  • GPS: geofence for mobile sites
  • Card: without smartphone for everyone

How teams usually mix

  • Hall: NFC or QR
  • Construction: QR + gate
  • Field: GPS geofence
  • Subs: physical cards
