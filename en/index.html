How it works

Physical card or app – you decide

Not every employee has a modern smartphone. Baupass Controll offers both: printed ID cards and digital passes in the app.

1

Receive card

Each employee gets a personalized ID card – printed and ready to use on site or in production.

2

Track time

Clock in at a terminal with the card or use the app. Both run on the same system.

3

Everything documented

Times, reports and documentation – centrally managed, including internal email.
