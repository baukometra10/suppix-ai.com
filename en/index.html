Features

One platform – full operations

Instead of separate tools for badges, attendance, documents and billing: everything in WorkPass – multi-tenant and auditable.

🪪

Digital identity

Photo, badge ID, QR, PIN, validity – physical card and app. Optional Apple/Google Wallet. Handover signature as legal proof.

🚪

Smart access

Turnstile, QR, NFC/HCE or GPS geofence. Gate live view with photo and alerts – no paper log.

📋

Live compliance

Expiring documents, assignment inbox, GDPR requests – alerts before risks grow.

📊

Reports & DATEV

PDF by email, DATEV CSV, daily close-out – ready for accounting and leadership.

🏢

Multi-company SaaS

Each company is an isolated tenant. Link subcontractors. Roles: Super-Admin, Company Admin, Gate, Worker.

🎨

Your branding everywhere

The default platform name is WorkPass. With your branding, company name and logo appear in the worker app, on badges and across the whole system.

📅

Deployment & daily plan

Create a monthly plan and send it to workers (email + push). In the app: daily plan with check-in, checkout and safety check.

💬

Chat · Call · Video

Internal communication in the employee app: chat with files and location, voice notes, calls and video conferences.

🔔

Proactive alerts

Late-start alerts, expiry warnings, plan delivery and email notifications – the system reports before you have to ask.

🤖

Ops · Camera · AI

Cameras/RTSP, automation rules, Copilot and emergency/roll-call – decisions without manual chase.

Care by the system

Not only control – real care for team and operations

WorkPass keeps you informed: who is coming, who is late, what is planned, and how to talk to the team safely and fast.

🛡️

Everyday safety

Safety checks in the daily plan, on-site guidance, live attendance and emergency/roll-call – so responsibility does not get lost in chaos.

📅

Work & deployment planning

Monthly deployment plan with site and times, sent to all workers by email and push. The app shows a clear daily plan.

💬

Internal communication

Chat between company and workers – including files, location, voice notes and read receipts. Right where the pass lives: in the app.

📞

Calls & online meetings

Direct voice calls and video conferences from the app – no extra tool when a short sync is needed.

Late-start alerts · who is expected and who is on site · expiring documents · deployment-plan delivery · PDF reports by email · push in the app. Operations feel cared for – not only “digitally logged”.
