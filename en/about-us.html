Our mission

Suppix AI UG stands for digital control with a human touch. With the WorkPass platform (white-label ready), we combine identity, access, compliance, workforce planning and team communication — via physical card or app.

🎯 Simplicity

Technology has to work in daily life — on construction sites, in factories and in the office. That is why we focus on clear interfaces and proven workflows.

🤝 Inclusion

Not everyone has a smartphone. Physical cards and digital passes complement each other and include every employee.

📊 Transparency

Time, access and documents documented clearly — for management, HR and project leads.

🚀 Continuous improvement

SUPPIX grows with your requirements — from visitor-badge mode to enterprise with API and integrations.
