Welcome to WorkPass – the digital identity and workforce platform by Suppix AI UG.

From paper and plastic cards to digital identity: access, compliance, live site view and team care in one system.

Every employee receives a personal ID card – physical for scanning or digital in the app. Employees without smartphones use the card. Mobile workers use WorkPass directly on their phone.

The default name is WorkPass. With your branding, company name, logo and pass name appear everywhere – in the app, on badges and in reports.

Deployment plans, chat, calls and alerts keep you close to the team. WorkPass Lohn connects the platform to payroll – from hours to the approved payslip.
