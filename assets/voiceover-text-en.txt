Welcome to Baupass Controll – the digital time tracking platform by Baukometra.

Whether on a construction site, in a factory or in the office: Baupass Controll lets you track working hours simply, reliably and in compliance with regulations.

Every employee receives a personal ID card – physical for scanning or digital in the app. Employees without smartphones use the card. Mobile workers use BauPass or KontrollPass directly on their phone.

For construction companies, the pass is called BauPass. For industrial businesses, KontrollPass – available in different designs to match your brand.

The dashboard gives you a real-time overview: attendance, projects, teams and reports – all in one place.

The integrated email and document system makes Baupass Controll a complete platform for your company – no extra software required.

Flexible plans from visitor card to enterprise – scalable for teams of any size.
